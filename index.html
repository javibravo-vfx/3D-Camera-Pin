BackplateLab
BackplateLab

Driving plates and car green-screen shots. Start from the set data (height, tilt, pan, lens, camera) and pins to match the camera of the plate, line up a car scan (OBJ or Gaussian splat), build the street behind it with moving traffic, work out the bokeh and Z defocus the lens should give, and render a previs.

            3D Assets

            Each viewport shows a piece of the environment (translucent violet). Import a better model for it (OBJ, GLB / glTF or FBX, or drop it on the viewport): it is turned and fitted to the pivot of the piece, then you adjust it. The 3D render uses them later, lit by an HDRI and a sun.

            3D Render

            The environment of 3D Camera Pin | Environment, lit by an HDRI and a sun, seen from the shot camera (Render, right), from the top to place the sun and from a free camera (left). For now the pieces are the environment's own (the assets of 3D Assets come next), and the cars are always the car pack, not the simple boxes. These settings are for any engine; each engine has its own section below (Raster, Path trace).

            Output

            Raster

            The usual engine of the Render view: fast and interactive. These settings are only for Raster (Max quality, Shadows and Quality are also at the top of Render).

            With Scene reflections on, the bounce comes from the same capture: Global illumination and its intensity are not used.

            Path trace

            The other engine of the Render view (at the top of Render, or Engine in 3D Render): light that really bounces, glass, soft shadows and depth of field, and slower. These settings are only for the path tracer; Pause and Reload are at the top of Render. Render still, Render sequence and the playblast use the engine of the view.
